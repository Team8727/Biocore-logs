What am I looking at?

Energy

Energy is battery voltage × supply current, added up only while the robot is enabled. It is shown in watt-hours (Wh).

Supply current

The battery-side current a device draws. It is the right number for power budgeting; motor-side stator current is not used here.

Sag

A sag starts when voltage drops below 9.0 V and lasts at least 100 ms. It ends after voltage recovers above 9.2 V.

Peak draw

The highest 100 ms average current or power. A short 100 ms window avoids reporting a single noisy sample as a peak.
